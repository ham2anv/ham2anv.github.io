Caleb

he/him

Total: 150 points

Background

Caleb was always a distant, “sensitive” child. His powerful magical aptitude was discovered early, and he was taken as an apprentice years before he was of age to enter the Academy. He learned many useful spells to assist in enchanting minor magic items for his master.

But Caleb was also given leave to explore his master’s and the Academy’s libraries for independent study. Through the many tomes, he learned of the many invisible truths of Yrth, such as spirits, gates, and banestorms. He was fascinated and longed to explore all of the secrets the world had to offer.

At the Academy, Caleb met his only close friend, Antoni. The two have talked about opening a magic item shop together, but Caleb doesn’t know how to tell his friend that he doesn’t intend to stay in Arvey. Instead, he thinks Antoni should open his shop with Miranda and have a happy life together.

Caleb knows that Kenlee also wants to see the world. He would very much like to travel with her, but he’s not been able to work up the courage to ask.

Gillaine and Caleb get on well enough, both being devoted students. He thinks she can be a little loud when out in groups, however.

Caleb sees how Jensen treats Antoni, and he hates the other young man for it. But his natural reserve and awkwardness keep him from helping his friend.

Goals
  • Get Antoni to understand why you must leave Arvey.
  • Convince Kenlee to leave with you.
  • Get Antoni to choose Miranda over Jensen.
