Jensen

he/him

Total: 150 points

Background

To the professors at the Academy, Jensen was a bright, if unambitious, pupil. That’s because his true talent lies in an area the Academy doesn’t cover much: Mind Control spells. These he has studied extensively on his own, sometimes at the cost of his other subjects.

In his personal life, Jensen is the consummate schemer. He uses his natural looks and unnatural charm to manipulate others and get what he wants. He pushes others and uses their discomfort to make himself look better.

Jensen sees Antoni as a mark and leverages the other man’s (obvious to Jensen) attraction. He hopes to insert himself into Antoni’s business to siphon away his family’s money.

Miranda and Jensen are rivals for Antoni’s affections. Neither has told him that they slept together during one of the couple’s break-ups.

Jensen finds Caleb unnerving but can’t put his finger on why. He is equally curious about and repelled by the man.

He is friendly and generous toward Gillaine, plying her with drink to get her to divulge gossip about everyone else. He gave Caleb notes on Gillaine’s project, knowing he would pass them on to Antoni.

Jensen has definitely slept with Kenlee. Surprisingly, he caught legitimate feelings for her, and he doesn’t know what to do with them. He knows she has feelings for Antoni, and that makes him treat the other man even worse.
