Antoni

he/him

Total: 150 points

Background

Antoni is the son of a wealthy merchant. Attractive, rich, and well liked, but only mildly talented and a poor student, he excels more in business affairs than magic. His confident demeanor masks a deep fear of inadequacy. With his training now complete, he wants to start his life without relying on his family’s wealth or connections, to prove that he can make it on his own.

Antoni and Caleb plan to open a shop together. Caleb will enchant inexpensive magic items, and Antoni will manage sales and finances. Before they can begin, Antoni must get Caleb to sign a contract formalizing the partnership, so he can secure a storefront from the Merchants Guild.

Miranda and Antoni have been on-again-off-again lovers for two years. While they are currently on-again, Antoni has recently been dwelling on his attraction to Jensen, which he believes is mutual.

Gillaine accuses Antoni of stealing her idea for a final project. The two have been rivals for some time, and the halfling believes that the similarities in their work go beyond coincidence. Antoni doesn’t know where she got this idea and has publicly denied any wrongdoing.

Antoni secretly despises Kenlee, but says nothing because of her status. For her part, Kenlee has never taken the hint and thinks she and Antoni are great friends.
