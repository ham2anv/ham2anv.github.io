Background

Miranda was born into a wealthy family, but her parents hate mages, so when her magery was discovered, they disinherited her. Luckily, she was talented enough to earn an apprenticeship and eventual admission to the Academy, but she carries both resentment and doubt to this day.

She is in love with Antoni, but her negative attitude and fear of commitment have made their relationship very rocky. She worries that if they break up again, he’ll end up with Jensen, whom she hates.

Miranda also hates Kenlee, who reminds her of everything she’s lost. And she knows that Kenlee also has feelings for Antoni, so at times she’s used their relationship to torture the other girl.

Unlike Kenlee, while Miranda competed fiercely with Gillaine in class, she actually deeply respects the young halfling woman. She thinks they could be friends now that they’ve graduated, if Miranda could just get out of her own way.

Caleb has confided his desire to leave Arvey with Miranda, and she supports him. The two are trying to figure out how to tell Antoni.

Goals
  • Kick Jensen out of Antoni's life once and for all.
  • Convince Antoni to let Caleb goes his own way.
  • Make friends with Gillaine.
